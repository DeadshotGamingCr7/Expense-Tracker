Data and accounting rules

This website uses your existing Transactions tab and a Transfers tab.

  • Income and expenses affect the selected account (UPI / bank or cash).
  • ATM withdrawals move money from UPI / bank to cash. Cash deposits move money from cash to UPI / bank.
  • Transfers are not income or expenses.
  • The manually typed ACC BALANCE column is ignored.
  • Balances are calculated from the opening balances and entries dated on or after the tracking start date.
